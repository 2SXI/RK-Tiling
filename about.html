Our story

Trading as Leghorn Investments since 1999.

R & K Hardware and Construction Co. was established to fill a gap in the construction and hardware supplies sector in Bulawayo. Built on operational excellence, quality products and superior customer service, we've spent over two decades growing a network of clients and suppliers that includes some of the largest government bodies and institutions in Zimbabwe — and as a woman-run company, we're proud to be pushing for greater women's representation in the construction industry.
