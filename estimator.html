How the tile estimate works

Add each room (width × length) or wall area (height × length) and the calculator adds them together. It then adds a 10% waste allowance for cuts and breakage and rounds up to whole packs, so you don’t run short mid-job.

Estimates are a guide. For a priced quote, or to confirm which tiles are in stock at our Bulawayo yard, contact us or browse our tile and adhesive range.
