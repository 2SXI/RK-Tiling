Frequently asked questions about hardware & tiling in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe

Opening hours, tile sizes, ceilings, adhesives and how to get a quote — answered.

Request a quote WhatsApp us

Where is R & K Hardware and Construction Co. located?

Our yard is at 15 Walsall Road, Bulawayo, Zimbabwe. You can find the map and directions on our contact page.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 7:30am to 5:30pm and on Saturday from 7:30am to 1:00pm. We are closed on Sundays.

What services do you offer in Bulawayo?

We supply building materials and carry out plumbing, painting (including waterproofing and damp-proofing), tiling and interior design, plus construction and renovation projects for homes, businesses and institutions.

Which tile sizes do you stock?

Our porcelain and ceramic floor tiles come in 40 × 40 cm, 60 × 60 cm and 120 × 60 cm formats. Packs contain between 2 and 12 pieces and cover 1.44 m² to 2.1 m². Current stock is confirmed on enquiry.

How many tiles do I need for my room?

Use our free tile calculator: enter the width and length of each room (or the height and length of each wall) and it estimates the tiles, packs, adhesive and grout you need, including a 10% waste allowance.

Which tile adhesive and grout brands do you sell?

We stock Rhino, Solid Adhesive and Ezee Tile adhesives in 20 kg bags, and grout in 5 kg bags in white and grey.

What PVC ceiling panels do you supply?

Our PVC ceiling panels come in 200, 300 and 400 mm widths and 6 mm to 8 mm thickness, in white, grey and wood or marble finishes, with bamboo, PVC and Kaylight cornice options and matching trim and fixings.

Do you supply materials only, or install as well?

Both. You can buy materials from our yard, or ask us to supply and install tiling, ceilings, plumbing and painting as part of a project.

How do I get a quote?

Call us on +263 78 161 8194, message us on WhatsApp, email rkhardware2013@gmail.com, or fill in the quote form on our contact page with your requirements and we will follow up.

Are you a registered contractor?

Yes. R & K Hardware and Construction Co. is a member of the Construction Industry Federation of Zimbabwe (CIFOZ), is registered with the Procurement Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (PRAZ) and is National Employment Council (NEC) compliant. We have traded since 1999 as a woman-run business.
